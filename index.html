The Second Floor

A classic tavern room, made for mingling.

Exposed brick, worn wooden floorboards, a low ceiling and warm chandelier light. It feels like a proper old pub, cozy enough for 15 and lively with 60. Perfect for a relaxed standing party where people dance, chat and gather around the bar.

Good to Know

Before you book.

A few house rules so your night goes smoothly.

Music by the house

The venue runs the music. Outside DJs and personal playlists aren't allowed.

Upstairs, no elevator

The room is reached by stairs and isn't wheelchair accessible.

Private events only

No promoted or ticketed events, and no wedding ceremonies.

Dress code

Come as you are, just nothing offensive or distasteful.

Easy parking

Free street parking, paid lots nearby and bike racks outside.

Cozy setting

No air conditioning or natural light, so it's best for evening parties.
